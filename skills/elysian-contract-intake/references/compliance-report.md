# Compliance Report Format & Decision Rules

Read this when running Phase 8.

## Before reviewing

Use the uploaded documents as the source of truth. Start by identifying the operative contract chain and the form revision dates.

Don't treat a blank or unsigned section as a defect until you have determined that the section is still operative. Later counters, buyer counters, amendments, and accepted addenda often make earlier sections irrelevant.

## What to check

- **Execution.** For every operative document, check:
  - buyer and seller signatures, with the correct number of parties
  - initials wherever the form version requires them
  - a date next to each signature
  - that names match across all forms
- **Completeness.** Check for:
  - a missing price, EMD, or financing term
  - a missing closing date or deadline dates
  - missing contingency details
  - a missing property ID
  - missing attorney info
  - an addendum, rider, or disclosure that is referenced but not attached
- **Consistency.** Check that the following match across the operative documents, unless a later signed document clearly overrides them:
  - price
  - dates
  - concessions
  - inclusions and exclusions
  - financing
  - escalation vs. final price
  - names
  - address
- **Sequencing.** Watch for:
  - signatures dated suspiciously out of order
  - an unsigned counter that the deal relies on
  - abandoned negotiation paths mixed in with the final terms
- **Disclosures.** Look for:
  - the NYS Agency Disclosure (Buyer/Seller)
  - the NYS Housing & Anti-Discrimination Disclosure
  - the Property Condition Disclosure Statement
  - the lead-based paint disclosure (pre-1978 homes)
  - buyer representation / compensation agreements

  Classify each one as:
  - required and missing
  - likely required / not found
  - possibly applicable / can't confirm
- **HETPA.** Check whether attorney approval and contingency language is present and consistent.
- **Deadlines.** Watch for contingency deadlines that fall after closing, overlap impossibly, or land on dates that conflict with each other.

## Severity

**Critical:**
- a signature missing on an operative document
- a material term blank on the controlling agreement
- a controlling addendum is missing
- the controlling path can't be determined
- operative terms conflict and the conflict is unresolved
- parties are mixed up or incorrect
- a version conflict creates material uncertainty

**Non-critical:**
- formatting or clerical issues
- a blank unused non-operative section
- an obsolete prior path is included, but the final path is clear

## Overall status

Pick exactly one:
- Compliance Ready
- Needs Correction Before Approval
- Major Deficiencies / Not Ready
- Needs Attorney / Senior Review

## Report structure

Use these sections in this order:

1. **File Summary.** Transaction type, the documents found, form versions and revision dates, and the controlling document path.
2. **Overall Compliance Status.**
3. **Critical Deficiencies.**
4. **Non-Critical Corrections.**
5. **Signature / Initial Review.** What is present, what is missing, and what is not required because of the hierarchy.
6. **Contract Logic / Override Analysis.** Which document controls, which terms were superseded, and why earlier blanks do or don't matter.
7. **Missing or Referenced-But-Not-Found Documents.**
8. **Human Reviewer Notes.** Ambiguities, version concerns, and suspicious sequencing.
9. **Document Decisions.** Approve or deny per Brokermint document, with a reason for each denial.
10. **Final Action List.** A clean checklist of what has to happen next, and who owns each item.
